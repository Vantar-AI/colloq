# Code of conduct

## The short version

Argue with the work, not the person. Assume the other side has a reason. Say what you
mean, in public, and be willing to be wrong in public too.

## What is expected

- Technical disagreement is the point of an RFC. Disagree precisely: name the claim, say
  why it fails, and say what evidence would change your mind.
- Say "I do not know" when you do not know. A project that publishes its failed gates
  cannot punish people for uncertainty.
- Review the change that was proposed, not the one you would have written.

## What is not accepted

Harassment, insults, discrimination, sustained disruption, or publishing someone's private
information. Persistent bad faith, meaning arguing without engaging with the answers,
counts too.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **hello@vantar.xyz**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept
private. Responses range from a correction to a permanent ban, depending on severity and
repetition. The maintainers hold themselves to this document as well.
